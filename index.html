I am an alchemist. My work is to perceive the true essences of alchemy — and to draw them out, stroke by stroke: the circle, the square, and the cord.

The circle is the cycle of heaven, wealth in endless flow. The square is the boundary of order — knowing how much to gain, and how much to keep. The cord is connection — stringing together what is scattered, mending the broken flow.

The White Coin represents the manifest, flowing and growing; the Black Coin represents the hidden, grounding and holding. One initiates, the other preserves. Together, they complete the alchemy of fortune.

For those who resonate, let us weave this cord together: print, textiles, jewelry, starlight ceilings, food packaging, NFTs.

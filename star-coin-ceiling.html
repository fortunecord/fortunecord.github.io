Star Coin Ceiling

Transform your ceiling into a starry night with FortuneCord's unique “star coin ceiling” line art. Each pattern replaces traditional stars with circular coins – floral, fruit, furry, and gem – connected by a cord. Perfect for automotive starlight headliners, architectural ceilings, and luxury interior design.

Why Star Coin Ceiling?

  • ✓ Original black and white line art – no color, only pure design.
  • ✓ Each coin motif (floral, fruit, furry, gem) replaces a star point.
  • ✓ A cord runs through every coin, symbolizing connection and flow.
  • ✓ Ideal for fiber optic starlight headliners, car roofs, and architectural ceilings.
